Privacy Policy – Horror Shorts Bot

Last updated: 21 October 2026

What the app does

Horror Shorts Bot is a personal automation tool used only by its owner. It generates short fictional videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Google / YouTube data

The app requests only the YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload). It uses this permission solely to upload videos to the owner's channel. It does not read, store, sell or share any other Google account data, and it does not access other users' data.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Users can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Data stored

The app stores only an authorisation token, kept in encrypted secret storage, and a list of the titles and video IDs it has uploaded.
